Overview

IndustryShapes is a novel benchmark dataset tailored for 6D object pose estimation in industrial settings. Targeting the challenges of textureless objects, reflective surfaces, and complex assembly tools, this dataset provides high-quality RGB-D data with precise annotations to advance the state of the art in robotic manipulation.
